About Our Hostel

Dedicated to Clean & Welcoming Living

A balanced residential atmosphere designed for students and working professionals.

"Radhe Krishna Boys & Girls Hostel offers a comfortable and convenient stay in the heart of Kankarbagh, Patna. Designed for students and working professionals, the hostel focuses on a clean, welcoming and convenient living experience."

Situated at A-20, P C Colony Rd, the hostel provides immediate connectivity to local transport, libraries, study centers, and daily essentials across Patrakar Nagar and Kankarbagh.
